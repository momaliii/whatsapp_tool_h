Drip sequences — timed follow-ups

Send a series of messages over time (day 0 / 3 / 7…) measured from when each contact is enrolled. Enroll your contacts list and watitool sends each step when it's due — while connected and within your sending window. If "stop if they reply" is on, anyone who replies is removed.

Tip: enable the sequence and connect WhatsApp — due steps go out automatically. Use {{name}} and {{track:CODE}} in step text.
